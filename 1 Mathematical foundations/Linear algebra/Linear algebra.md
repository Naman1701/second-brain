# Linear algebra

This note collects the core linear algebra ideas used in wireless communication.

## Key ideas
- vectors and matrices
- eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- matrix multiplication and inversion
- rank and orthogonality
- singular value decomposition (SVD)

## Why it matters in wireless
Linear algebra is central to:
- MIMO channel representation
- beamforming
- precoding and combining
- spatial multiplexing
- channel diagonalization
