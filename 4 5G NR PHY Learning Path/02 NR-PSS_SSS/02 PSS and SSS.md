# 02 PSS and SSS

PSS and SSS are used for synchronization and cell search.

## Main ideas
- primary synchronization signal
- secondary synchronization signal
- cell identity detection
- frequency and time synchronization

## Why it matters
Before higher-layer processing, the UE must identify and synchronize with the serving cell.
