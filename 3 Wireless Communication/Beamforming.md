# Beamforming

Beamforming focuses the transmit or receive energy in a preferred spatial direction.

## Main ideas
- analog beamforming
- digital beamforming
- hybrid beamforming
- beam management

## In 5G
Beamforming is especially important for:
- mmWave operation
- massive MIMO
- spatial selectivity
- improved link reliability
