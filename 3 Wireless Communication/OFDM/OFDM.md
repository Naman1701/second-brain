# OFDM

OFDM is a multicarrier modulation technique widely used in wireless systems, including 4G and 5G.

## Core idea
The available bandwidth is divided into many closely spaced orthogonal subcarriers.

## Benefits
- simple equalization in frequency-selective channels
- efficient use of spectrum
- compatibility with MIMO and advanced scheduling

## Related topics
- CP-OFDM
- SC-FDMA
- subcarrier spacing
- resource grid
- guard interval

## In 5G
OFDM and its variants are central to NR waveform design and scheduling.
