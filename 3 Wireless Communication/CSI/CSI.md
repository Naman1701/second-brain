# CSI

CSI stands for channel state information.

## Meaning
CSI describes what the transmitter and receiver know about the current communication channel.

## Typical quantities
- CQI
- PMI
- RI
- channel matrix estimates

## Why it matters
CSI is crucial for:
- link adaptation
- beamforming
- scheduling
- precoding design
- codebook-based feedback
