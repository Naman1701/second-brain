# Complex baseband representation

This note explains the common complex representation used in modern wireless communication.

## Key idea
A passband signal can be modeled in a lower-dimensional complex form centered at the carrier frequency.

## Why it matters
It simplifies:
- modulation
- channel representation
- demodulation
- MIMO processing
- OFDM analysis

## Important concepts
- I/Q components
- analytic signal
- passband vs baseband
- complex channel coefficients
