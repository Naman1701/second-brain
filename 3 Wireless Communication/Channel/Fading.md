# Fading

Fading describes rapid variations in signal amplitude and phase due to multipath and mobility.

## Types
- large-scale fading
- small-scale fading
- flat fading
- frequency-selective fading
- slow fading
- fast fading

## Important metrics
- coherence time
- coherence bandwidth
- Doppler spread
- delay spread

## Relevance to 5G
Fading is a central issue in NR design, affecting:
- link adaptation
- coding and modulation choice
- CSI reporting
- beam management
